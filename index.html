CircuitMate
Hardware & Dev Ecosystem · Gemma 4 vision

Got an Arduino kit and an idea? Let's wire it.

Tell CircuitMate what you want to build, or snap a photo of the parts on your desk. Gemma 4 identifies the components and returns a parts list with Indian prices, a pin-by-pin wiring table, complete Arduino or MicroPython code, test steps and safety notes. It can also spot mistakes in a photo of your breadboard.
